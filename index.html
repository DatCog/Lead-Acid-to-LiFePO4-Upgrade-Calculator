Engineering & Charger Conversion Tips

⚠️
Charger Compatibility Alert: Do not use traditional Lead-Acid chargers with automatic desulfation or equalization modes on LiFePO4 batteries. Ensure your marine/RV charger supports a dedicated Lithium profile (14.2V - 14.6V absorption).
💡
BMS Low-Temp Protection: Charging lithium below 32°F (0°C) can cause irreversible lithium plating. Choose LiFePO4 cells with integrated Low-Temperature Cut-Off protection if operating in freezing marine or winter environments.
⚡
Zero Voltage Sag Performance: Unlike Lead-Acid (which drops rapidly under heavy load), Lithium maintains flat voltage output under draw, keeping marine winches, thrusters, and AC inverters running at peak efficiency.
